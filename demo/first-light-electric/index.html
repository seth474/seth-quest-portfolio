About First Light Electric

First Light Electric Inc. has served the East Hampton community with quality electrical work for years. Technicians are trained to safety standards and focused on dependable service.

Whether the job is a new installation, a repair, or a larger upgrade, the work is built around clear communication, clean job sites, and workmanship that meets New York regulations.

  • Professional and punctual
  • Transparent and competitive pricing
  • Responsive communication
  • Clean and safe work environment
  • Compliance with NY electrical codes
