House watching

You lock up in September. We keep watching.

Every week through the off-season we walk your house top to bottom, then send you a text and photos.

  • Water: no leaks, no drips, nothing under the sinks.
  • Heat and pipes: heat running, pipes safe from freezing.
  • Air: rooms aired out so nothing turns musty or moldy.
  • Storms: checked before and after every big one.
  • The outside: doors, windows, gutters, roof and yard.
