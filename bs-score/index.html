01 — How it works

Three steps on the watch page.

  1. 01

    Open the video

    Stay on the YouTube page you were already going to watch. Desktop youtube.com.

  2. 02

    Check BS Score

    The button sits under the title. It sends the title, description, captions, and visible comments to the scorer.

  3. 03

    Read it, or skip ahead

    A meter, a verdict, red flags, and skip-to-the-meat chapters. The score is on screen either way.

02 — The panel

The card the extension shows.

Same meter, verdict, red flags, and chapter jumps as the side panel. This sample is the open fixture, scored by the heuristic that runs when no AI key is set.

Example only. It is not a rating of a live channel, and it is not a promise about every video.

03 — Free and open

No paywall. No account required to see a score.

  • Free to use. The score shows up without a subscription or a payment.
  • Open source. The extension files are public. Read the manifest.
  • Heuristic by default. With no AI key on the server, the grade is a fixed rubric. An optional key can ask Grok instead. Neither path invents a Chrome store listing or a user count.
