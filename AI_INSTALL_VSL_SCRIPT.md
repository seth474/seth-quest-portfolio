# COMPLETE AI — two 30–45 second VSL scripts

Offer on screen and in the words: COMPLETE AI. All in on AI in 10 days. A done-for-you AI install for busy service business owners (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fuel, home services). $4,800 total: $2,400 down, $2,400 on day 10. Guarantee: we'll create at least $10,000 in value for your business, or you get a 100% refund. Optional upkeep after the included 30 days of support is $300 a month and never renews automatically. Only 4 installs a month. Seth does them personally. Two meetings: kickoff and handoff.

Centerpiece to say out loud: your new full-time AI employee works 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, never calls in sick, and costs about $20 a month to run. The client pays the software. A part-time human employee is $2,000 or more a month.

Value stack, if a lower-third is used. Do not read every line unless the take is running short. The anchors add to $12,500:

- 24/7 AI agent employee (answers leads, texts back missed calls): $3,000
- Instant lead follow-up: $1,500
- Estimate and quote follow-up: $1,500
- Review requests: $1,000
- Morning numbers text: $750
- Google profile fixes: $1,000
- Owner's personal AI assistant, setup and training: $2,000
- 2 meetings, kickoff and handoff: $750
- 30 days support: $1,000

Say the total as about $12,000+ in value, then the price: $4,800.

Say the guarantee in both scripts: we'll create at least $10,000 in value for your business, or you get a 100% refund.

The refund fine print stays on the page and in the FAQ. Do not say it in these spots unless the take runs short: if they take the refund, everything built is removed, including the ads, automations, AI assistant, and custom website.

Read each script out loud once before the shoot. Spoken words only, not the stage directions, should land between 30 and 45 seconds. Do not add a second pitch.

The sitcom piece uses the garage-and-fence shape of a home-improvement sitcom. Do not use that show's character names, faces, theme, logo, or catchphrases. The neighbor is a friendly guy in a plain t-shirt and ordinary jean shorts. No flirting, no body jokes, no close-ups of the shorts.

## 1. Seth, direct to camera

Aggressive. About 42 seconds. One setup. Eye line into the lens. Brisk, not shouted.

**Seth:** Complete AI. All in on AI in ten days.

If you're not using AI agents, you're missing the boat. Now is the time.

Too busy to figure it out? I'll install your full-time AI employee in ten days. Twenty-four hours a day. Never calls in sick. About twenty dollars a month to run. A part-timer is two thousand a month.

Forty-eight hundred. Twenty-four hundred down. Twenty-four hundred on day ten. Worth twelve thousand five hundred. You pay forty-eight hundred.

We'll create at least ten thousand in value for your business, or you get one hundred percent of your money back. Four spots. Book it now.

## 2. Garage and fence, then Seth

About 40 seconds of dialogue, plus the short pauses in the garage. Funny, then the offer. The joke is the missed calls, not the neighbor.

### Script

**Neighbor,** leaning on the fence: Phone's ringing.

**Contractor,** from under the sink: I'm under the sink.

**Neighbor:** She hung up. Called the other guy. He already texted her.

**Contractor,** sliding out, looking at the phone: That was a water heater.

**Neighbor:** And a panel. And two "can you come this week." You missed all four.

**Seth,** to camera: Too busy to figure out AI? I'll install Complete AI in ten days. Your employee works twenty-four seven, never calls in sick, about twenty dollars a month. A part-timer is two thousand. Forty-eight hundred. Twenty-four hundred down, twenty-four hundred on day ten. We'll create at least ten thousand in value, or you get one hundred percent of your money back. Four spots. Book it now.
