# All In on AI in 10 Days — two 30–45 second VSL scripts

Offer on screen and in the words: All In on AI in 10 Days. A done-for-you AI install for busy service business owners (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fuel, home services). $4,800 total: $2,400 to kick off, $2,400 on day 10. If the owner is not 100% satisfied, they do not pay the second $2,400. Optional upkeep is $300 a month and never renews automatically. Only 4 installs a month. Seth does them personally. Two meetings: kickoff and handoff.

What is installed: missed-call text-back, instant lead replies, estimate follow-up, review requests, the owner's own AI assistant, a morning numbers text, and Google profile fixes.

Comparison to say out loud: a part-time employee doing this busy work costs about $2,000 a month, $24,000 a year. The AI team is $4,800 and works 24/7.

Read each script out loud once before the shoot. Spoken words only, not the stage directions, should land between 30 and 45 seconds. Script 1 is about 100 spoken words. Script 2 is about 93, with short pauses in the garage scene. Do not add a second pitch.

The sitcom piece uses the garage-and-fence shape of a home-improvement sitcom. Do not use that show's character names, faces, theme, logo, or catchphrases. The neighbor is a friendly guy in a plain t-shirt and ordinary jean shorts. No flirting, no body jokes, no close-ups of the shorts.

## 1. Seth, direct to camera

Aggressive. About 40 seconds. One setup. Eye line into the lens. Brisk, not shouted.

**Seth:** If you're not using AI agents for the repetitive busy work, you're missing the boat. Now is the time.

Too busy running your business to figure out AI? I'll install it for you in ten days.

Missed-call text-back. Instant replies. Estimate follow-up. Review requests. Your assistant. Morning numbers. Google profile, fixed.

A part-timer costs two thousand a month. Twenty-four thousand a year. Your AI team is forty-eight hundred, and it works twenty-four seven.

Twenty-four hundred to kick off. Twenty-four hundred on day ten. Not a hundred percent satisfied? Don't pay the second twenty-four hundred.

Two meetings. Four spots. Book it now.

## 2. Garage and fence, then Seth

About 42 seconds. Funny, then the offer. The joke is the missed calls, not the neighbor.

### Script

**Neighbor,** leaning on the fence: Phone's ringing.

**Contractor,** from under the sink: I'm under the sink.

**Neighbor:** She hung up. Called the other guy. He already texted her.

**Contractor,** sliding out, looking at the phone: That was a water heater.

**Neighbor:** And a panel. And two "can you come this week." You missed all four.

**Seth,** to camera: Too busy to figure out AI? I'll install it in ten days. Text-back, replies, follow-up, reviews, your assistant, morning numbers, Google profile. Forty-eight hundred. Twenty-four hundred now. Twenty-four hundred on day ten. Not satisfied, you don't pay the rest. Your AI team works twenty-four seven. A part-timer is twenty-four thousand a year. Four spots. Book it now.
