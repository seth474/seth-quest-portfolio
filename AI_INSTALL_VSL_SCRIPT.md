# AI Install — two 30–45 second VSL scripts

Offer on screen and in the words: AI Install for service businesses (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fuel, home services). Valued at $10,000. $5,000 total: $2,500 deposit, $2,500 in 30 days once it is installed. If the owner does not think it was worth it at 30 days, they do not pay the second half. Optional upkeep is $300 a month and never renews automatically. Only 4 installs a month. Seth does them personally.

What is installed: missed-call text-back, instant quote and lead replies, estimate follow-up, review requests, the owner's own AI assistant, a morning numbers text, Google profile and website fixes.

Read each script out loud once before the shoot. Both are written to land between 30 and 45 seconds at a natural pace. Do not add a second pitch.

The sitcom piece uses the garage-and-fence shape of a home-improvement sitcom. Do not use that show's character names, faces, theme, logo, or catchphrases. The neighbor is a friendly guy in a plain t-shirt and ordinary jean shorts. No flirting, no body jokes, no close-ups of the shorts.

## 1. Seth, direct to camera

About 40 seconds. One setup. Eye line into the lens.

**Seth:** Your competitors are switching to AI. You're losing the job while you call them back.

They call while you're on a roof or in the truck. Voicemail. They hire whoever texts back.

I install that. Missed-call text-back. Instant quote replies. Estimate follow-up. Review requests. Your own assistant. A morning numbers text. Google and your website, fixed. HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fuel, home services.

Worth ten thousand. You pay five. Twenty-five hundred now. Twenty-five hundred in thirty days. If it wasn't worth it, you don't pay the rest.

Four a month. I do them myself. Book your install.

## 2. Garage and fence, then Seth

About 42 seconds. Funny, then the offer. The joke is the missed calls, not the neighbor.

### Script

**Neighbor,** leaning on the fence: Phone's ringing.

**Contractor,** from under the sink: I'm under the sink.

**Neighbor:** She hung up. Called the other guy. He already texted her.

**Contractor,** sliding out, looking at the phone: That was a water heater.

**Neighbor:** And a panel. And two "can you come this week." You missed all four.

**Seth,** to camera: I install the text-back, the replies, the follow-up, the review ask, your assistant, the morning numbers, and the Google and website fixes. Worth ten thousand. You pay five. Half now, half in thirty days. If it wasn't worth it, you don't pay the rest. Four a month. I do them myself. Book your install.
