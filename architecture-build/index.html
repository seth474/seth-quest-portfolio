Seven homes in ten months

Glamping development near Austin, Texas. Co-foreman on two shipping container homes, the Monarch and Spyglass treehouses, a geodesic dome, a cocoon and a safari tent, all with plumbing and AC. Hands-on framing, plumbing, electrical, waterproofing and installation.

02 · Onera · Container homes

Catching a problem early

The containers arrived with fiberglass insulation, a condensation and mold risk in Texas with AC running. It came out within the hour, and the team switched to closed-cell spray foam.

03 · Monarch Treehouse

Frame up in days, not months

I helped build it and identified Vulkstrut, a prefab system of CNC-made light-gauge steel, so the frame went up in days, not months. That mattered on a treehouse, where everything takes substantially longer above the ground.
