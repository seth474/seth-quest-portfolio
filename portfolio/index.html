A limited portfolio of my work in lighting, furniture, interiors, products, and architecture.

I started my career with lighting and furniture design in San Francisco. During this time I founded an office design consultancy to provide ergonomic furniture and innovative interiors to startups.

I used rapid prototyping to develop products, became an inventor, and was on the founding team of Factory, an innovation consultancy that worked with Fortune 500 companies. After being burnt out from tech, I founded Waterhouse, building a community of 1000+ members with spa focused events.

My degree is in product design, so I’ve included some of the notable products I’ve developed over the years. In this chapter of my life I’ve focused my attention on architecture. I’m currently utilizing all of my skills as an industrial designer to bring beautiful, carbon neutral homes to the marketplace. My mission is to create affordable contemporary homes for the millennial generation.
