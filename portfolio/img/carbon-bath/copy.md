# The Carbon Bath

**Concept.** Current design: v47f.

## Intro

The Carbon Bath is a concept: an expanding bath and shower that travels on the hitch of a mid-size SUV. The Carbon Bath travels with any mid-size SUV. Far easier than pulling a trailer. Four rings nest for the drive; parked, it stands on four landing legs, the rings rise, and one wall slides open onto a teak floor with a rain shower, a sink and a toilet. These images are 3D renders of the current v47f model, not photographs. The design is in development.

## Section captions

### Hero
The Carbon Bath, deployed at sunset, with the sliding door open.

### Travel
The Carbon Bath travels with any mid-size SUV. Far easier than pulling a trailer. Shown packed on a Lexus GX 550 for scale.

### It expands
Four rings nest. One wall slides. Landing legs swing out from under the floor; the rings telescope up; teak stairs fold out from a slim stair box under the floor (Parts List v47f: 20.78 × 25.30 × 1.75 in); then the door wall slides open.

The 00–05 sunset extension sequence from deck5 is not used here. That sequence, and the Bath 01 deck page that labels it “Packed, 33 in” / “Full height, 84 in ceiling,” shows an earlier model (the deck itself is marked MODEL V37). The frames in this set are taken from the v47f deploy animation so the sequence matches the current design.

### Inside
A teak-floored room with a rain shower, a sink and a pill mirror, and a toilet cabinet. Walk in through the sliding door wall.

### Details
Teak stair treads folded out from the stair box. A light bar set into the trim ring under the floor. Packed underside: the landing-leg posts and the stair box under the floor plate.

### Concept
The Carbon Bath is in design development. All images are 3D renders of the v47f concept model, not photographs; the person in the hero is composited. The Lexus GX 550 is shown for scale only and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lexus or Toyota.
