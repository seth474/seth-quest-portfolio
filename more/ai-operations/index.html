FAQ

Questions owners ask

Is this going to replace my staff?

No. The point is to take repetitive admin off your team so they can spend more time on the work you hired them for. Agents draft. People decide.

How is this different from chatting with AI?

Chat can answer a question. An agent can do a task in your own email, files, and spreadsheets, then stop for a person to review. We set that up with your team and leave written steps they can run.

Can AI do all the admin in my business?

No. Some tasks fit. Some need a person. Some aren't worth the setup. Part of the four weeks is sorting which is which.

Do you guarantee a certain number of hours saved?

No. What you get depends on your tools, your process, and whether your team uses the workflows. We find the admin worth taking on, build it with you, and make sure your team can run it.

What tools do we need?

Most shops already have enough to start: email, shared files, and spreadsheets. Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. If something else would help, we'll say so, and you decide.

What will the software cost?

For simpler setups, about $20 a month, and typically $20–$50 per month depending on the tools and how much you use them. Billed to you, in your name.

Is our data safe? What can the AI see?

Only what you allow. We set that tool by tool. Read-only where it can be. Nothing important goes out until a person approves it. The accounts stay in your name.

We use a CRM or field software. Will this work with it?

It depends on whether that software can connect. We'll look on the call and tell you what can be done, what needs a workaround, and what isn't worth doing.

My team isn't technical. Will they use this?

The sessions are with your team, on tasks they already do, in tools they already open. They leave with written steps.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. It's four weeks. When it's done, what we built is yours.

What if we're not a fit?

Then we'll tell you on the call.
