The old way

You ask ChatGPT something. You copy the answer. Then you go do the work yourself.

The new way

You give an AI agent a task. It works through the steps using the tools you've allowed it to use (your email, your files, your spreadsheets), then it stops so a person can review before anything important goes out.

What an agent can help your team do

  • Summarize and organize long email threads
  • Prepare customer follow-ups and replies as drafts
  • Find and organize business documents
  • Build reports from information you already have
  • Prepare estimates and proposals for review
  • Update trackers and keep jobs moving
  • Turn a repeated task into a process anyone on the team can run

You stay in control

You choose what it can access. Read-only where it should be, write access only where it needs it.

You approve what goes out. Agents draft. People send.

Questions Owners Ask

Is this going to replace my staff?

No. The point is to take repetitive admin off your team's plate so they can spend more time on the work you hired them for. Your people stay in charge: agents draft, people decide.

Can AI automate everything in my business?

No, and anyone who says so is selling something. Some tasks are a great fit, some need a person, and some aren't worth automating. Part of the work is figuring out which is which.

Do you guarantee a certain number of hours saved?

No. Results depend on your tools, your processes and how your team uses the workflows. What we do is find the admin work worth targeting, build it with you and make sure your team can run it.

What tools do we need?

Most businesses already have what's needed to start: email, shared files and spreadsheets (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365). We'll recommend any additional software during the engagement, and you decide whether to add it.

What will the software cost?

For simpler setups, typically around $20–$50 per month, depending on the tools and usage. It's billed to you directly, in your name.

Is our data safe? What can the AI access?

Only what you allow. We set permissions tool by tool, read-only wherever possible, and build in review steps so nothing important goes out without a person approving it. Accounts stay in your name, under your control.

We use a CRM or field-service software. Will it work with that?

It depends on whether your software offers an integration. We'll check on our first call and tell you plainly what's possible, what needs a workaround and what isn't worth doing.

My team isn't technical. Will they actually use this?

That's why we work directly with your team in weekly working sessions. Everything is built around tasks they already do, in tools they already use, with written instructions they can follow.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. It's a four-week engagement. On completion, everything we built is yours.

What if we're not a fit?

Then we'll tell you on the call.
