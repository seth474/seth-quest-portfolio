Growth roadmap

Prepared for Dunemere Management · October 2026

The first milestone is 5 new member clients, with no set deadline. The plan brings leads to Ryan instead of having him cold call: look credible online, ask the people who already trust him, then get found on Google.

Ideal client

Owns a $2M+ home (often $5–15M) in East Hampton or Amagansett, has several homes and little time, wants one person to call, and communicates fairly.

Not a fit

One-off "check it when I call" hourly work, or owners who argue over every visit.

How the leads come in, in order

1

Look credible first (this block)

New website with real photos, the correct phone number and easy booking. Every other channel sends people here.

2

Ask the people who already trust Ryan (this block)

A "we're growing" email, then a personal call, to every current and past client asking for introductions. His clients know other owners of $5M homes.

3

Brokers Ryan already knows (this block)

A handful of calls to agents who know Ryan, with the new website as the reason to reconnect. A bigger realtor list can come later, once calls are working.

+

Hamptons Adventure customers (this block)

Ryan already has a list of e-bike customers, and many own homes out here. One personal email from Ryan, after the new site is live, tells them about Dunemere.

4

Get found on Google (next block)

Google Business Profile plus the first 5 reviews from current clients.

5

Later, once the basics work

Instagram consistency, the weekly sales-report system, a small Google ads test, and HubSpot automation if it's still needed.

Who does what

Seth

Ryan
