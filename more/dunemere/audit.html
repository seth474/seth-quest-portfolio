Dunemere's sales funnel, scored

Prepared for Ryan Struble, Dunemere Management · October 5, 2026

A funnel has two halves. Leads: how owners find you. Sales: what happens once they reach you. We scored 10 parts of each from 0 to 10. Dunemere is strong once an owner is in the room, and nearly invisible before that.

Lead score13 out of 100 pointsHow owners find you

Almost every new client comes from word of mouth. Every one of the ten ways owners find a company like yours scores 5 or less.

Target after this block: 35 points
Sales score33 out of 100 pointsWhat happens once they reach you

You close 80% of the owners you meet and keep them for decades. The steps before the meeting lose people: wrong number, no booking, no proof.

Target after this block: 70 points
