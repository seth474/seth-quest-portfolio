The screenshots

Each category card above lists the numbered notes that show the problem.

Homepage, top of the page

1

The phone button dials the wrong number

It shows (631) 223-8966 but calls 631-233-8966. Anyone who taps it reaches a stranger.

Fix: correct the link today.

2

Stock photo, not a Hamptons home

A generic living room, and on a phone it loads blank for several seconds first.

Fix: one real photo of Ryan at a client home.

Stats section

3

Stats that aren't true

"850 Protected Homes," "57 New Clients," "35 Experience," and a typo, "Employes." Dunemere looks after 9 homes. A prospect checking references will notice.

Fix: replace with real proof: clients of 20 years, same-day replies.

2

Another stock house

Show the team instead: the crew and trucks in front of a real home says "18 people" better than a number.

Testimonials

4

Testimonials no one can check

Stock portraits, names that can't be verified, and two of three quotes are about rental bookings, which isn't the service Ryan sells.

Fix: 3 real quotes from long-time clients, first name and town, with permission.

Footer, on every page

5

The company name is misspelled

"DENUMERE" appears in the footer heading and the copyright on every page.

Fix: correct it sitewide.

7

A claim that contradicts itself

"The most refined, economical, and efficient" service. Refined and economical pull in opposite directions, and neither says who Dunemere is for.

6

Social icons go nowhere

They link to facebook.com and similar home pages, not Dunemere accounts.

Fix: remove them until real accounts exist.

Fix list, in order

  1. Today: fix the phone link, the "DENUMERE" misspelling, and remove the fake stats and dead social icons
  2. This week: a real photo of Ryan and real client homes; three real testimonials
  3. Session 2: launch the new site with one clear action, "Book a free home check," and a fast-loading page
  4. Next: a page for each service, a question-based FAQ, and a Google Business Profile with reviews
