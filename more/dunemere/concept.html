Why membership

We don't do one-off visits. Here's why.

You can't look after a house you see twice a year. Seeing it every week is what makes us useful.

We learn your house

The gate code, the corner of the basement that takes on water after a heavy rain, the gutter that clogs every November. You only learn those things by being there every week.

We catch it early

A bedroom that smells musty after two months shut up. A hairline crack that wasn't there last week. Small things stay small when someone notices them on time.

No meter running

One flat monthly fee means you never have to wonder whether a phone call is worth it. Call us whenever you have a question. That's what we're here for.

Meet Ryan

It started with one family and one oceanfront house.

In his early twenties, Ryan Struble was asked to keep an eye on a family's oceanfront home. He walked it every week, checked for leaks and made sure the heat stayed on. Over the years they asked him to do more: manage contractors, open the house in spring, close it in fall. Then they introduced him to family and friends.

That's how Dunemere grew, one referral at a time. Ryan grew up in East Hampton and brings more than 15 years in real estate and 10 years in plumbing and home improvement. Some of his clients have been with him for 20 years.

Questions

Good to know.

What does it cost?

Membership is $300 a month from September through June, covering weekly checks and reports. As a member, small tasks are $125 an hour and larger projects are quoted after a walkthrough, with the scope in writing before work starts.

Can you just check on my house now and then?

We don't offer one-off or on-call visits. Weekly visits are how we learn a house well enough to take responsibility for it. If you want someone who knows your home, membership is the way to start.

Can you manage a project if I'm not a member yet?

For larger projects, yes. Call Ryan. Most project clients become members, because the house still needs looking after once the work is done.

What happens if you find a problem?

We call or text you right away with photos, fix small issues ourselves, and bring in a trusted contractor for anything bigger, with your approval first.
