Bonus: website audit

Website score: 23 out of 100 points

For a referral business, the website's job is to confirm Ryan is the real deal. It does the opposite: a phone button that dials the wrong number, the company name misspelled "DENUMERE" on every page, made-up stats ("850 Protected Homes," "18 Employes"), stock photos, stock-portrait testimonials, and a claim to be both "refined" and "economical." An owner of a $5M home won't sign up from it.

What we agreed: 10 hours, three things

A 10-hour starter block at $75/hour ($750). Referral milestone: once Dunemere signs 5 new clients, Ryan introduces Seth to other business owners he knows.

1

New website

About 80% built, on a new position: "Few homes. Full attention." Why these words. Next step: a photo of Ryan leaning on his truck in front of a client home. We'll add simple "Dunemere Management" lettering to the truck door in the photo: clean and professional, not a wrap.

See the new website preview

2

HubSpot email to ~500 brokers: proposing we defer it

  • HubSpot doesn't allow emailing purchased or scraped lists. It risks the account and your email domain.
  • Setup with automation is its own project, usually $1,500–2,500, not something that fits in 10 hours next to a website.
  • Emails to 500 strangers are a slow way to 5 clients.

Instead, use the same hours on referrals:

  • Ask your current and past clients for introductions. Seth writes the email and what to say; you make the calls.
  • Call a few brokers you already know, and send them the new website as the reason to reconnect.
  • Email your Hamptons Adventure customer list once, from you, about Dunemere. Many of them own homes here. See the draft
  • Keep a simple list of who you called and what they said, so nothing slips.

One idea to discuss

Referral fee for the trades you work with

A pool company, landscaper or contractor you trust may service dozens of homes. If they introduce an owner who signs up, you pay them a set thank-you, for example $500. They already have the relationships; this gives them a reason to mention you.
