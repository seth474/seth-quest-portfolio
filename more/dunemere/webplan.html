Site structure

Separate pages, each answering one thing clearly, because that's what Google and AI assistants find and quote: Home · House watching in East Hampton & Amagansett · Project oversight · About Ryan · Questions · Book a visit · From the field (monthly stories from real jobs).

Questions the site answers (draft for Ryan to edit)

Q

Who can watch my house in East Hampton while I'm away?

Dunemere Management. East Hampton native Ryan Struble and his team walk second homes from Southampton to Amagansett every week, inside and out, and send a photo report after every visit.

Q

How much does house watching cost in the Hamptons?

Membership starts at $300 a month, September through June [tiers by home size to be decided]. Small tasks for members are $125 an hour; larger projects are quoted after a walkthrough.

Q

What does a house watcher check each week?

Doors, windows and locks; heat; water and leaks; roof and gutters from the ground; pool and spa; yard, drainage and storm damage; lighting; alarm, mail and deliveries.

Q

Can you manage a roof replacement or renovation while I'm away?

Yes. We get the bids, hire the contractor, check the work every week and send photo updates, always in the owner's interest.

Q

Do you do one-off or on-call visits?

No. Weekly visits are how we learn a house well enough to take responsibility for it. Every client starts with membership.

Proof that makes it real

  • A real photo of Ryan, for example with his truck in front of a Hamptons home
  • Real numbers: clients for 20 years, same-day response
  • A sample weekly report with real, anonymous photos
  • Three real testimonials with first name and town
  • Walk-and-talk videos from real jobs (Ryan narrates, no addresses or faces, owner's permission first)

Decisions before launch

  • Pricing: a flat $300, or three tiers by home size or complexity, shown as "from $300/month"
  • Main offer: "free home check" or "book a consultation"
  • Phone number: keep the current one or get a new business number; it must match everywhere
